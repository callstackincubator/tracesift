Give the call tree the timeline's cut, place a timeline box by its duration rather than its offset, and cut culprits at a fixed 15 ms.

A nested box on the timeline said `278 ms from 550 ms, 0 ms of its own`, under a parent reading `678 ms from 394 ms`. Both offsets are measured from the start of the task, but two rows of a chart stacked that way read as one inside the other, so the pair invites a reader to subtract — and 550 ms is not where that call began inside its parent. Only the outermost box names its start now. Everything nested gives the figure it is there to give: `278 ms, 0 ms of its own`.

The call tree can now be read with React and the engine collapsed away, which is the cut the Timeline has drawn by since it was added, applied to the rows instead of the boxes. Opening a task on the full tree means scrolling past `processRootScheduleInMicrotask`, `(anonymous)` and ten more frames before the first name a reader recognises; on the 3.2 s task of the local trace the collapsed view puts `Search_Search`, `Search_SearchTypeMenuWide` and `SidebarOrderedReportsContextProvider` on the first screen. Dropping a frame lifts its children into its place and charges the time it burned in its own body to the nearest frame kept above it, exactly as the timeline does, so a parent still accounts for its own time plus its children's and the two views agree on the frames they share. The full tree stays the default: it is the only view that still holds everything the profiler recorded, and it is the one that can answer how a frame was reached when the answer runs through the reconciler.

Culprits are now cut at 15 ms of self time or 15 ms including callees, rather than at one percent of the task. What a reader can act on does not scale with the block it sat in, and the share floor said the opposite at both ends: 2 ms rows on a 219 ms task, and on a 3.2 s one a 32 ms cut that hid most of what could realistically be fixed. Admitting a frame on its inclusive figure too is new, and it puts back the rows a self-time ranking structurally cannot have — a frame with 2 ms of its own and 400 ms through its callees is where the time went, however little of it burned in its own body. Self time still orders the table, so the frames that burned it stay on top.

The cap on that table rises from 24 to 60, because at 24 it bound before the floor did and silently undid half of it. On the 3.2 s task of the local trace 36 frames clear 15 ms of self time and another 24 clear it only through their callees; since self time sorts above inclusive time, a cap of 24 meant not one delegating frame ever reached the table. The slots cost nothing — a culprit serialises to about 1.4 kB against a task tree that runs to several megabytes in the same response — and the floor, not the cap, is what should decide which frames a reader sees. The hand-off keeps its own limit of 24, because that list is prose pasted into another agent's context where a long tail dilutes the rows above it rather than adding to them, and the remainder line still accounts for every millisecond the list leaves out. A card still shows eight rows, and its footnote now says how many more Explore can name rather than leaving the remainder as a dead end.
